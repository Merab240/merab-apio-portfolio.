Mera Retail Sales & Customer Analytics

Turning retail sales data into actionable business insights.

This is a personal portfolio and practice project focused on analysing retail sales data to understand revenue, profit, customer activity, sales channels, and location performance. The analysis uses Excel for initial data preparation, PostgreSQL/SQL for structured querying, and Power BI for interactive reporting and visualisation. The Power BI dashboard is the primary deliverable completed to date.

Project Objectives

The analysis is designed to answer the following business questions about retail performance.

  • How much revenue and profit were generated overall?
  • Which sales channels performed best?
  • Which locations generated the most revenue?
  • How did sales change over time?
  • What recommendations could improve sales performance?
